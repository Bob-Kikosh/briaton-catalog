          About the store

          Surface-mounted lights can be installed on a ceiling, a floor or a wall to match your design concept. They work well in almost any space: offices, shopping and business centers, stores, restaurants and hotels.

          Our catalog offers a wide range of surface-mounted lights in different shapes, colors, sizes and styles, and the filters make it easy to find the right model. If you are choosing lights for a specific project, contact our managers: they will prepare a professional lighting plan, pick the equipment and arrange delivery. We supply fixtures for decorative, accent and flood lighting with various light sources, with the largest selection among LED models.
